Do you use your phone while lying on your side? When you do, the screen turns to face the wrong way. Sideways Mode fixes this by making the screen face sideways so that it's aligned with your head. See the video for a demonstration. Permissions • Modify system settings: To change screen orientation and system auto rotation • Small app overlay (Sony users only): So you can use a small app to change screen orientation (This app was formerly called "Auto Rotation for Lying Down".)
