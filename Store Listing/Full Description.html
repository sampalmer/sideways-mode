Do you use auto screen rotation? Do you use your phone or tablet while lying on your side? When you do, the screen rotates and faces the wrong way. This app makes auto rotation work properly when you're on your side. The screen still rotates automatically, but it's 90 degrees off from normal. Permissions • Modify system settings: To change screen orientation and system auto rotation • Draw over other apps / display system-level alerts: To override other apps' screen orientations • Small app overlay (Sony users only): So you can use a small app to change screen orientation (This app was formerly called "Auto Rotation for Lying Down".)
