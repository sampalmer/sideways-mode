Sideways Mode accesses the following information from the device it is installed on in order to provide its core functionality:
* Device orientation

Sideways Mode may also access the following information from the device it is installed on in order to provide its optional "Incompatible apps" feature:
* Installed apps
* Running apps
* App usage statistics

Sideways Mode stores some of the above information on the device it is installed on. Sideways Mode does not transmit any information off the device it is installed on.
